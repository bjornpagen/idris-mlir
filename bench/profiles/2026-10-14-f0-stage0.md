# F0 record: the stage-0 self module through IdrSimplify (2026-10-14)

Proposal 0003's F0 (= 0005's S0, 0001's G0), measured on arm64 macOS
at 44495199 plus the InlineCleanup refactor noted at the end. The
corpus is the frontend's stage-0 dump preserved from the stage-1
attempts: 58,236 functions, 294 MB of generic-form text (the frontend
took ~140 s to write it). Method: `idris-mlir-opt` with explicit pass
pipelines and `-mlir-timing`; one Simplify round is
`buildSimplifyRound` spelled out (effects, loop-breakers, round-facts,
the per-function nest, inline, specialize, SCCP, per-function
IntRange, SymbolDCE), validated against `idr-simplify{max-rounds=1}`
(447 s wall composite against 340 s manual; the difference is the
composite's fingerprints, tree-timing and parse/print accounting).
Raw timing trees live with the session's artifacts, not here
(bench/profiles/README.md: traces are not committed; the numbers are).

## The prefix is cheap

Parse 11.7 s, isolate 3.0 s, deduplicate 3.8 s, contify 3.5 s, print
5.0 s; 27.0 s total, 1.09 GB peak RSS. 294 MB in, 158 MB out,
100,198 functions after isolation outlines closures.

## Round 1 (composite, tree timing, user/wall seconds)

IdrSimplify 592/447. IdrInline 350/330, of which the post-inline
cleanup pipeline 244/233 and its largest single instance 213/211 —
one function's InlineCleanup, effectively serial. SCCP 43/43 serial.
Analyses inside the round: CallGraph 11.9, References 6.0+2.7,
InlineDecisions 4.1, BindingTimes 3.0, RoundFacts 3.4/3.1, Effects
7.1, LoopBreakers 7.3 (user seconds, mostly serial). The per-function
step is healthy: 77 user-seconds in 10 wall. IntRange nested: 48/6.
SymbolDCE 9.4. The loop's own fingerprints and bookkeeping ~4 s.
Peak RSS 5.27 GB.

## Sixteen rounds, no fixpoint

Round walls (manual series, seconds): 340, 119, 99, 117, 97, 142,
100, 120, 127, 143, 133, 119, 121, 113, 118, 117. Module bytes:
158 MB -> 244 MB, still changing at round 16 (the first shrink).
Operation count ~471k -> ~1.25M by round 14; function count falls
100,198 -> 28,276 (round 6) as inlining absorbs bodies. Largest
functions after round 6: IdrisMLIR.MLIR.statementText.spec1 ~152k
ops (14% of the module in one function), Idris.ProcessIdr.processMod
.lam4 ~76k, Libraries.Utils.Path.parse ~45k. Steady-round split is
flat: IdrInline dominates, SCCP is 21-27 s serial every round,
canonicalize+CSE ~20 s, IntRange ~28 user-seconds, SymbolDCE ~5 s.
Peak RSS ~5.8 GB. Extrapolated Simplify alone: >2,000 s and unfinished
-- the round count is the multiplier, and it does not decay.

## SCCs, before and after inlining

Direct `idr.call` graph (closure applies are not edges in it):
at emission 58,235 functions, largest SCC 174 (TTImp.Elab), 93%
singletons; after isolate/dedup/contify largest 14; after six rounds
largest 24 of 28,276, 99 functions in any SCC over 10. The SCC
pipeline of 0003 F3 will not starve on one component. Namespace
locality, for the partition question: 17% of distinct origin-to-origin
direct-call pairs share an Idris module, 49% a top-level segment.

## Once at the fixpoint, and the tail

RemoveDeadValues{canonicalize=false} + SymbolDCE on the round-16
module: 65 s wall (liveness 51 s), 7.65 GB peak, -11 MB. The tail
(defunctionalize onward) cannot be timed on this module: after 16
rounds defunctionalize still refuses a runtime closure Simplify had
not resolved (`unsupported (runtime closure)`, Idris.Parser.directive
.lam4) -- the fixpoint is load-bearing, not ceremonial. LLVM's share
remains measured only at test scale (3.8% of the
every-interfaces-export profile, bench/profiles 2026-10-13).

## The InlineCleanup refactor (proposal 0003 §2.1)

Landed with this record: simplifyRegions deleted from the hot
cleanup (once per touched function in a new parallel structural pass
after inlining), the unreachable sweep conditional and
non-recursive, two whole-body walks deleted. Strict worklist
confinement (ExistingAndNewOps) was implemented, measured (a do
block: 19 rounds against 3; a linearity-invalid intermediate state
on this corpus) and reverted the same day -- recorded in the code.
A/B, one manual round on the round-6 module, old against new binary
under identical background-QoS conditions: the hot/cold split measured 7% slower old-against-new under identical throttled conditions (462 s against 494 s on the round-6 module; IdrInline user time 108 s -> 139 s; outputs equal in size, 18 lines of op order apart) and was reverted, as was strict worklist confinement (a do block at 19 rounds against 3). Both are recorded in proposal 0003 §2.1..

## Thresholds this record fixes (0003 §6)

- F2 lands when preserved analyses remove at least half the measured
  per-round analysis rebuild (~25 user-seconds in round 1) at
  identical bytes.
- F3 lands when the SCC pipeline's Simplify segment on this corpus
  beats 600 s wall to the same fixpoint the rounds reach (against
  >2,000 s and unfinished at 16 rounds), with revisits recorded.
- Any future round-structure change is judged on rounds-to-fixpoint
  first: a cheaper round that needs more rounds has bought nothing
  (§2.1's rule, now with the 19-round data point behind it).
