- **The arcs (step 4).** For each walked `(W, edges)`, each `(s, T, o)` in edges, and each argument `(j, _, t)` of s, the argument lands at `(T, o + j)`:
  - **A named position** `(T, o + j)`, with its first naming `(s, j)` (a `SortedMap Position (Site, Nat)`, the first kept).
  - **A direct arc** `(Just (W, i), (T, o + j), weigh i t, (s, j))` for each i in `variablesIn t` whose `weigh i t` is not `Absent`. `variablesIn` lists every `Var i _` anywhere, deduplicated, a `Choice`'s matched-on template included (`Leaf`, `Token` and `Held` hold none), and leaves the decision to `weigh`. `Absent` is no arc; the arc is made with its `Present` proof as below.   - **A leading arc**, for each edge whose site's reach is `Dispatches t r k` (template, the dictionary's `Root`, method index) and whose offset a is above 0. M is the callee node, a method function whose first a parameters are J's (§4.6 step 4).
    - J ranges over `roots fl W t` (G8 reads the dispatch's root r there; you never match on r), keeping those whose `Fields` arity is a and that call M: `lookup (J, M.definition) calls` is a site. That site, J's own call of M, is how a J is told to be M's implementation, with no reading of a field's head.
    - Every walked node of J whose heads equal M's heads below a (`filter ((< a) . fst) M.heads`) gives one arc per p < a: `(Just (J-node, p), (M, p), Finite 0 0, wrote)`. Every source joins the positions.
    - wrote is `(that site, p)`: J's own call of M, whose argument p is J's parameter p, so a message reads it in J's binders.
    - No J kept is `internal fc "a dispatch reached a method function that no root of it calls"`. `reaches` took M from one of these roots, and Idris writes a method's field as a lambda that applies the method's own function (`Terms.idr:991-997`).
    - Why the arc is needed: J's own call of M passes its method dictionaries as runtime values, with no roots, so it reaches another node of M (*read*, the soundness review: `Terms.idr:1001-1026`). Without the arc, M's leading positions under the dispatch get no arc, and nothing bounds them.
  - **A projection write** for each `(f, w)` in `fieldsIn t` (G1's: f a `Field` or a `Held`, w its arc weight) and each root J in `roots fl W f`: the arc `(q, (T, o + j), w, (s, j))` for every position q whose node's definition is J, made once the positions are known (step 7).

- **K and A (step 5).** K is the largest of `height t` over every argument of every walked edge, capped targets included, and of the gain g over every `Finite g 0` that `fieldsIn` gives (each field's e + 1).
