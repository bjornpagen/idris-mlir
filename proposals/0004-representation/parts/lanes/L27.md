**Fixed decisions:**
- **Layout.** `build/self/src` is a fresh copy of `compiler/src` for each
  stage (`rm -rf`, `cp -R`, `rm -rf build/self/src/build`), at that one path
  (locations carry it). `build/self/stageN/` holds:
  `idris-mlir-front.core` and `idris-mlir-front.mlirbc` (the frontend's),
  `frontend-timing` (its `--timing` file), `frontend-stdout`,
  `frontend-stderr`, `frontend-kib`, `ttc/` (a copy of `build/self/src/build`
  taken after the frontend), `pipeline-stdout`, `pipeline-stderr` (ending
  with `-mlir-timing`'s report), `pipeline-kib`, and `idris-mlir-front` (the
  executable). The compared artifacts keep their names.
- **The frontend half** of stage N, run in `build/self/src`: the frontend
  is `$idris_mlir_front` for stage 1 (what `make build` made, the frontend
  Idris built, on Chez) and `$self/stage$((n - 1))/idris-mlir-front` after;
  `peak_memory "$out/frontend-kib" "$timeout_cmd" -k 10 "$frontend_limit" "$front" --prefix "$checkout_prefix" -p idris-compiler --timing "$out/frontend-timing" --core "$out/idris-mlir-front.core" -o "$out/idris-mlir-front.mlirbc" IdrisMLIR/Frontend/Driver.idr > "$out/frontend-stdout" 2> "$out/frontend-stderr"`.
- **The pipeline half** of stage N: the driver is `$idris_mlir` for every
  stage, as today; `peak_memory "$out/pipeline-kib" "$timeout_cmd" -k 10 "$pipeline_limit" "$idris_mlir" --num-threads="$ncpu" -mlir-timing "$out/idris-mlir-front.mlirbc" -o "$out/idris-mlir-front" > "$out/pipeline-stdout" 2> "$out/pipeline-stderr"`,
  with no `--prefix` and no `-p` (the driver refuses them for a module).
  The driver never writes or removes its module input, so a failed
  pipeline leaves the module for the next attempt.
- **Limits**, two variables at the top, each with its authority in a
  comment: `frontend_limit=1800`, about five times the 345 s the self
  compile's frontend step took on Chez (`bench/profiles/2026-10-17-self-growth.md`,
  the typecheck machine), and debt for stages 2 and 3, whose native
  frontend has never run; `pipeline_limit=7200`, debt: the pipeline has
  never run on the self module (0003's S3 measures it), and 0003's margins
  (the label solve 10 s, the optimizer 600 s) leave LLVM and the link
  unmeasured. The grace before a kill, `-k 10`, is the one
  `harness/testutils.sh` gives every test run; the last 30 lines of a failed
  half's stderr are today's. No other number is added.
- **Failure.** With `$timeout_cmd` empty the script stops at once with
  `$timeout_missing`, as `bench/run.sh` does. A half that exits non-zero
  prints `stage N: <frontend|pipeline> exit S in T s, peak K KiB`, then,
  for 124 or 137, `timed out after <limit>s`, and the last 30 lines of its
  stderr, and the run exits 1. A half that succeeds prints the same line
  with its exit 0.
- **The comparison** at 3 (`last` at least 3): `cmp` of
  `idris-mlir-front`, `idris-mlir-front.mlirbc` and `idris-mlir-front.core`
  between stage 2 and stage 3, then `diff -rq "$self/stage2/ttc" "$self/stage3/ttc"`;
  any difference prints `error: stage 2 and stage 3 differ in <artifact or
  path>` and exits 1; else `stage 2 = stage 3: self-hosting holds`. Stage 1
  is never compared (its frontend runs on Chez: 0003 §5).
- **The header comment** says what a stage is now, the layout above, the
  limits' meaning, and that `tools/self.sh 1` is S1 and S3 on the owner's
  machine (the frontend's record, then the pipeline's).
