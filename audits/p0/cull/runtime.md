## 2. The frame stack is a home lifetime: `runtime/Alloc/Frames.cpp`

- **Part**: `runtime/Alloc/Frames.cpp:1-123` (`idris_rt_frame_push`,
  `idris_rt_frame_pop`, their chunk chain and spare), the two helpers
  `Spec/Runtime/Helpers.idr:211-216`, their lowering calls
  (`foreign/idr/lib/Conversion/Cells.cpp:188-196, 237-239`,
  `Lower/Drops.cpp:172, 305`).
- **Fact it supplies**: a thread-global LIFO bump allocator over a chain of
  heap chunks, each at least twice the last, pushed by a bump and popped by
  putting the top back. **Where the representation already holds it**: the
  runtime's lifetime chains are the same mechanism. A `home k` lifetime
  (D93) is a thread-global cursor in a static array of home cursors
  (`Lower/Lifetimes.cpp:103-120`, `Lower/Statics.cpp:102-116`), bumped by
  `idris_rt_arena_chunk` (`runtime/Alloc/Arenas.cpp:98-108`); a stack's end
  gives back the chunks past a mark and makes the mark the cursor again,
  `idris_rt_stack_reset` (`Arenas.cpp:128-134`). `frame_push(bytes)` is
  `bump(homeSlot(k), bytes)` and `frame_pop(frame)` is `stack_reset(frame)`
  and a store of `frame` into that slot. D31 asks for "one stack per
  result type" (`proposals/0000-mlir-native.md:585-586`), which
  `Frames.cpp` is not (its `first`, `top`, `limit`, `newest`, `spare` are
  one set of globals, `:31-38`): a home site per result type is.
- **Rule that once required it**: D31 (frames on a stack of their own) and
  C63 ("frame stacks give chunks back, one spare kept",
  `proposals/0000-mlir-native.md:3773-3774`). Neither names a second
  allocator; the spare is a policy, which `stack_reset` should then keep
  too (a `.stack` lifetime opened in a loop at a chunk's edge has the same
  alternation C63 answered for frames).
- **Readers**: `git grep -n "helper::framePush\|helper::framePop"`: 4
  (Cells.cpp ×2, Drops.cpp ×2); tests: none by name.
- **Deletion**: `Frames.cpp` whole and `runtime/CMakeLists.txt:67`; the two
  helpers in `Helpers.idr` (and, regenerated, 4 lines of `idris_rt_ops.h`,
  6 of `RuntimeAbi.h.inc`); the four calls become the home bump and the
  stack reset the lifetime lowering already writes; `Arenas.cpp` gains the
  spare (about 10 lines) and `Statics.cpp` one home per frame family. About
  135 lines out, 20 in: net about 115.
- **Verdict**: MERGE into the lifetime chain (`Arenas.cpp`, `home k`).
  Confidence: read for the identity of the two mechanisms; conjecture for
  the cost (a push today reads two globals, a home bump loads the trailer
  word at the cursor and stores it past the cell: one load and one store
  either way, unmeasured). An architectural choice (which part holds the
  fact; D31 and D93 are rulings), so it goes to the owner before it is
  written.
- **Owner**: L11 (runtime), L10 (frames, `Conversion/Frame.cpp`), L21
  (`Cells.cpp`, `Lower/`), L05 (lifetimes). Waits on: the owner's ruling.
