# Cull audit: the frontend and Emit (`compiler/src`)

HEAD at start: `363dcc0d` (2026-10-23). HEAD at finish: `d378e120`. Between the two,
only `Emit/Operations.idr` changed under `compiler/src` (C282, the `Given`
parts and `givenHold`), and no finding below depends on that change. Line
numbers are at `363dcc0d` unless a row says otherwise. For `Emit/Operations.idr`
they are at `d378e120`.

Read first: AGENTS.md (the rule "Delete before you optimize", 2026-10-23), the
representation-first skill, testing-by-search (for the one test this audit
touches, `tests/dialect-syntax`), P0 §2 (D46, D50, D51, D63, D68),
`recheck6/deletions-and-bugs.md` (N5 to N10, the L16 list) and
`handoffs/L16-fold-design.md` (C281's §4.3 deletion list).

Method and ground. The audit is read-only. Everything below was measured on a
`git archive` export of `363dcc0d` (`/tmp/frontend-cull/head`) with three
throwaway scripts:
- `imports.py`: module reachability from `IdrisMLIR.Frontend.Driver` and
  `IdrisMLIR.SyntaxSamples`, the two `main`s;
- `dead2.py`: every top-level name with no occurrence outside its own
  definition lines, searched over `compiler/src`, `compiler/spec`, `tests`,
  `harness` and `libs`;
- `live2.py`: declaration-level liveness from those `main`s and from the four
  tests that import frontend modules. A reference resolves only into the
  modules its file imports, transitively.

The scripts work on tokens, so they over-approximate liveness: a name shared
by two definitions keeps both alive. A row marked *measured* names the script
or the `grep` behind it, and every candidate was then confirmed by a `grep`
quoted in its row. As the user asked ("keep all the sources"), each claim
keeps its source (path:line, search or ruling), and nothing under `sources/`
is a candidate.

Out of scope by assignment: `Frontend/Growth/` and `Frontend/Growth.idr`
(13,500 lines), and `Frontend/Translate/` and `Frontend/Translate.idr`
(18,094 lines). C281 is redesigning them. They are listed once, as "goes
with C281", with no detail.

Checkout state. Other lanes are editing the files below, so a deletion that

## Candidates, ranked by what they delete

### 1. Two ways to build one `idr` op: the printed builder and `specOp`

- **The part.**
  - `compiler/src/IdrisMLIR/Dialect/Idr.idr` (generated by specgen, 1,042
    lines): 96 of its 166 builders have no reader.
  - The criterion that prints them: `Spec/Generator/Idris.idr:307-310`,
    `written (SomeOp op) = legalAt op.stages Higher`.
  - The second builder beside them: `Emit/Operations.idr:138-139` `specOp`
    (at `d378e120`), with `specGuarded` at `:113-119`.
  - The frontend's own enum layer that selects printed builders:
    - `Types.idr:436` `Shift` and its `Show` at `:569`;
    - `:477` `Numeric` and its `Show` at `:543`;
    - `Prim`'s constructors `IntShift`, `ToStr`, `FromStr`, `ToBig`, `FromBig`,
      and `Cast` of `DoubleToInt` and `… SChar` (`:487-509`), with their
      `Show (Prim k)` clauses (`:578-597`) and `slots` clauses (`:614-633`);
    - `Emit/Operations.idr:328-332` `shiftOp`, and the `prim` clauses at
      `:353`, `:362-366`, `:376`, `:379-385` and `:388-406`.
- **The fact it supplies.** How to make an `idr` op that takes no attribute
  and no region: its operands, its result types, and the guards a partial op
  needs. That fact is the specification's op value (`Spec.Op i o 0 0`: its
  `guards` and `typing`), and `specOp` reads it directly. The printed builder
  is a second rendering of the same value.
  - For the 96 dead builders there is no reader. Some of their ops the
    frontend already writes from the spec value through `specOp`: `bigAdd` at
    `Translate/Terms.idr:2171`; `natToBig` and `natFromBig` at `:2723-2730`;
    `strSubstr` at `Translate/Primitives.idr:147`; and the registry's
    `PureCall`, `IOCall` and `Builds` rows. Only C++ passes write the rest
    (`lifetime`, `lend`, `activation`, `lifetime.forget`, `data.tag`), or
    nothing does.
  - For the spec families the enum layer restates: `Spec/Ops/Scalars.idr:104`
    `shift : ShiftDirection -> Signedness -> Op 2 1 0 0` is `Shift` plus
    `shiftOp`; `Scalars.toChar sg` (`:162`), `Bigs.bigFromInt sg` (`:456`)
    and `Strings.strShowSint/Uint` (`:134-141`) are the other
    signedness-chosen clauses. `Spec/Ops/Numeric.idr:52` `ShiftDirection` is
    the closed set `Types.Shift` copies.
- **The rule that once required it.** D50: "Builders and encoders stay
  printed, each a plain function typed by its op's operand count … a generic
  builder's type would depend on a run-time value of varying representation,
  which the subset refuses." `specOp` is that generic builder, for every op
  with no attribute and no region. It is typed by the op value's own indices,
  and both hosts build it, because it is in the tree today. So D50's premise
  no longer holds for those ops. It still holds for ops with attributes or
  regions, which `specOp` cannot make. `written` was meant to say "the
  frontend writes this op", but what it states is "the op is legal at
  `Higher`".
- **Every reader.**
  - *Measured* (`dead2.py`, then `grep -rnw <builder> compiler tests harness`
    for each of the 96): 0 outside `Dialect/Idr.idr`.
  - Of the 70 builders that are used, 29 are attribute-less, region-less and
    of fixed arity: `numeralToNat`, `numeralOfNat`, `mayLoop`, `natToExtent`,
    `platformOs`, `intDivS`, `intModS`, `intShlS/U`, `intShrS/U`,
    `intToCharS/U`, `f64ToInt`, `natPred`, `bigFromSint/Uint`, `bigSmall`,
    `bigToInt`, `bigFromDouble`, `bigToDouble`, `strFromChar`,
    `strShowSint/Uint/Double`, `strBytesLength`, `strToInt`, `strToDouble`
    and `lazyForce` (each with an `Op` suffix). Their readers are
    `Emit/Operations.idr` and `Emit/Bodies.idr`.
- **Why a flag will not do.** A per-op "the frontend writes it" flag would
  drift. The same flag on admitted upstream ops is already wrong for five ops
  (candidate 7). The structural criterion cannot drift, because `specOp`
  makes exactly the ops that have no attribute and no region.
- **The exact deletion.**
  - `Spec/Generator/Idris.idr:307-310`: print a builder only for an op with
    an attribute, a region or a variadic group.
  - Regenerate `Dialect/Idr.idr`:
    - −582 lines for the 96 dead builders (*measured*: block count over the
      file);
    - −178 lines for the 29 used attribute-less builders (*measured*), whose
      calls become `specOp <spec value>`.
  - `Emit/Operations.idr`: `shiftOp` and the clauses listed above become
    `specOp` of the spec value. About 25 lines go.
  - `Types.idr`: `Shift` (replaced by `ShiftDirection`), `Numeric`, and
    `Prim`'s per-category constructors become `Op` values, together with
    their `Show` and `slots` clauses. About 40 lines go.
  - `Translate/Primitives.idr` picks spec values (C281's file).
  - `foreign/idr/generated.manifest`: the fingerprint of `Dialect/Idr.idr`.
  - `tests/dialect-syntax`: unchanged. `SyntaxSamples.idr` samples ops
    through builders of attributed ops.
- **Verdict.** MERGE into the specification's op value, read through
  `specOp`, for every op with no attribute and no region. The duplication is
  *read*; 582 and 178 lines are *measured*; the ~65 lines of `Emit` and
  `Types` are *conjecture*.
  - If the owner keeps D50 as written, the fallback is a DELETE of the 582
    dead lines alone. That needs a new specification fact (each op's writer,
    as `Admitted.writer` is for upstream ops), which is a copy that drifts
    (see "Why a flag will not do").
- **Owner, and what it waits on.**
  - Owners: L16 (`Dialect/Idr.idr`, `Emit`, `Types`), L03 (the generator),
    INT (the manifest).
  - It waits on the owner's ruling on D50 for attribute-less ops (a recorded
    design, so a reversal is the owner's).
  - `Emit/Operations.idr` is modified in the checkout.
  - `Translate/Primitives.idr` moves with C281.
