**Contract:** the grade is `!idr.graded<uses, hold, T>` (D88, D90): uses
on every type, hold only on types that hold references, `static` with no
sharing field; a word used freely is the bare type (C143); no hold is
ever undecided (C144). A lifetime is an SSA value (D63) of type
`!idr.lifetime<view | room | cursor | home k>` (D93); its memory source
is its opener (`idr.lifetime`, `.home`, `.block`, `.stack`, `.chain`);
`idr.activation` and `idr.lend` bind `view` (C145); `idr.lifetime.forget`
is the one op whose result is a lifetime; `idr.lifetime.reserve` (C146);
the pack and unpack pair (D95) that L10 applies at a framed call.
