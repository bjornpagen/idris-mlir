## Provenance

Fetched 2026-10-09 from `https://www.jsoftware.com/papers/rank.htm` and its framed files plus
the two stylesheets (read-only mirror, Python `urllib`). The paper gives an executable J model
of the rank conjunction (frames and cells, prefix agreement, permissive assembly, the zero
frame), the integrated rank support (IRS) speed-ups measured on JPC386 2.04, and the definition
of *uniform* verbs with rank and shape calculators.
